# Surface: Baligya Bukidnon app (Browse, Item, Post/Edit, My Listings, Login)

Mode: Operate. Visitor: a student anywhere in Bukidnon hunting a specific item in their size, or a seller posting one. Task first; phones, tablets and laptops all matter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every listing is issued like a campus ID card (a vertical lanyard ID: top strip, framed photo, labeled fields, one big number), so it reads as belonging to a student community. Refuses the default rounded-photo-card marketplace grid where price, size and school are an afterthought caption.

OWN-WORLD: Navy and white only (owner's revision): navy #1E3A8A strips and primary buttons, white card stock, navy-tinted neutrals (surface #F5F7FC, rules #DCE2EF, ink #0F1D45, muted #4B5A7D). One readable face, Geist at normal width, sentence-case labels; Geist Mono only for the listing No. Fields are small labels over bold values in ruled boxes. Every card has a slot punch in its strip and a No. Sold is a dark SOLD band across the photo. No green, gradients, glass or colored side borders; no university seal, crest or official marks.

STORY: A student sees at a glance which items fit (size, school, price all printed as fields), taps one, and messages the seller in one navy action, quoting the item No.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy header strip with the Baligya Bukidnon wordmark, nav and white Post Item button. Directly under it the search strip: a large white search field and a printed "Showing" line that restates the active filters and count. Category tabs row. Desktop: a left rail of labeled filter fields plus sort; phone: a Filters toggle. Then the grid of vertical ID cards (2 / 3 / 4 columns), price as the one oversized numeral on each.

FORM: Campus ID Card, position 7 of the grounded list, seed key 61b07cec. Raises: labeled segments with tabular figures (boarding pass); a quotable short No. per listing (teletext); state is palette law, navy = available/act, dark = sold (arcade); the price is the hull number (racing). Signature interaction: cards swing from their slot punch like an ID on a lanyard on hover/focus (reduced motion: none). Post page shows a live preview of the listing's ID card while you fill the form.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
