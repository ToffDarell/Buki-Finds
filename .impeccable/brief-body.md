# Surface: BukSU Market app (Browse, Item, Post/Edit, My Listings, Login)

Mode: Operate. Visitor: a BukSU student hunting a specific item in their size, or a seller posting one. Task first; phones, tablets and laptops all matter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every listing is issued like a campus ID card (a vertical lanyard ID: top strip, framed photo, labeled fields, one big number), so it reads as belonging to BukSU. Refuses the default rounded-photo-card marketplace grid where price, size and school are an afterthought caption.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned navy #1E3A8A strips, white/#F9FAFB laminated card stock, #E5E7EB printed field rules, ink #111827, green #10B981 only for the one act per screen (with ink text for contrast). Condensed grotesk (Archivo, narrow width) for strips, prices and field values; Geist for body; Geist Mono for the listing No. Fields are tiny caps labels over values in ruled boxes. Every card has a slot punch in its strip and a No. strip. Sold is a gray SOLD band across the photo. No gradients, glass or colored side borders; no university seal, crest or official marks.

STORY: A student sees at a glance which items fit (size, school, price all printed as fields), taps one, and messages the seller in one green action, quoting the item No.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy header strip with the BukSU Market wordmark, nav and green Post Item. Directly under it the search strip: a large white search field and a printed "Showing" line that restates the active filters and count. Category tabs row. Desktop: a left rail of labeled filter fields plus sort; phone: a Filters toggle. Then the grid of vertical ID cards (2 / 3 / 4 columns), price as the one oversized numeral on each.

FORM: Campus ID Card, position 7 of the grounded list, seed key 61b07cec. Raises: labeled segments with tabular figures (boarding pass); a quotable short No. per listing (teletext); state is palette law, green = available/act, gray = sold (arcade); the price is the hull number (racing). Signature interaction: cards swing from their slot punch like an ID on a lanyard on hover/focus (reduced motion: none). Post page shows a live preview of the listing's ID card while you fill the form.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
